questions

Things people ask.

Does she need to download anything?
No. The link opens in any browser — phone or desktop. Nothing to install, no app, no permission prompts.
Is any of this stored anywhere?
No. Everything is client-side. The only third party in the chain is the link shortener (is.gd, TinyURL, or spoo.me) which sees the long link long enough to shorten it.
Can she tell it's from me?
Yes — your name signs the reveal card, and if you set a Messenger handle, the contact chip and the yes-button point to your chat. The page is deliberately not anonymous.
When does the music play?
If she taps yes, the song starts immediately. If she taps no, the prank plays in silence, then the song starts the moment the reveal card appears.
What happens when she taps no?
Fake power-off sequence — black screen, slide-to-power-off slider, low-battery frame, boot animation. Then the reveal card appears, the song starts, and the no button dodges forever.
Can I use a custom short link?
Yes. Type an alias in the builder and it tries to reserve it on is.gd, then TinyURL, then spoo.me. If taken, it falls back to a random link.
What if she says no for real?
The no button dodges forever. She can chase it as long as she wants. The only way forward is yes.
After she says yes?
The song starts, the celebration screen appears, and she can share the page back to you on Messenger, Facebook, or TikTok.
